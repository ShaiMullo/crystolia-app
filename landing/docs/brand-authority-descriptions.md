Our products come in two main formats: 0.9-liter bottles for everyday home use, and 5-liter containers designed for restaurants, catering businesses, the food industry, and retailers. Crystolia canola oil is refined with a delicate taste, ideal for frying and baking. Our sunflower oil is pure and high quality, suited to every kitchen use.

What sets us apart is quality control across the entire supply chain — from the source to the end customer. Every shipment is inspected against strict standards, so our customers receive a consistent, reliable product with every order. Alongside quality, we are committed to fast, dependable delivery throughout Israel — on the agreed schedule and at the agreed price.

Crystolia serves a wide range of customers: households looking for quality oil for daily cooking, restaurants and caterers that depend on steady and reliable supply, retail chains, and food manufacturers. Our team operates in Hebrew, English, and Russian, and accompanies every customer personally — from the first inquiry to ongoing supply.

We believe good cooking oil is the foundation of every kitchen, whether at home or in a professional setting. That is why we commit to three simple things: consistent quality, organized supply, and reliable service.
